The information gap

A crisis is the hardest time to learn your rights.

Most people only learn how to respond when they urgently need to. The Unmuted helps women prepare earlier—before fear, pressure, or uncertainty makes every choice harder.

⏳
Before harm
Recognize warning signs, understand consent and boundaries, and learn which rights or protections may apply.
🧭
During harm
Understand your choices, contact trusted people, communicate with institutions or authorities, and act according to your situation.
⚖️
After harm
Document what happened, preserve relevant information, and find psychological, legal, or community support.

Emergency SOS
Prepare trusted contacts and a help message before an emergency. When needed, hold for two seconds to add your GPS coordinates and open the message on your phone.

Encrypted Evidence Vault
Capture or import photos, video, audio, and files. Each item is encrypted on your device with AES-256-GCM, labeled as live capture or later import, and fingerprinted with SHA-256.

Guided Incident Notes
Situation-specific prompts help you record only what you know. Safety and evidence-preservation guidance appears before the encrypted local note.

Verified Support Directory
Find mental-health hotlines, rights services, legal-aid centers, and specialist NGOs by city, with service hours and verification dates.

Before · During · After

Know what to do at every stage.

Learn and rehearse early, respond more clearly in the moment, and find documentation and support tools afterward.

1
Prepare before
Recognize harassment, understand consent and boundaries, learn which protections may apply, and prepare trusted contacts.
2
Respond during
Use what you practiced to understand your options, contact trusted people, and communicate with institutions or authorities.
3
Record and seek support after
Use encrypted documentation, guided notes, and verified psychological or legal resources according to what you need.

Support resources

From practice to real-world support.

Learning helps you prepare. If you or someone you know needs support now, these mainland China resources offer a practical next step.

🇨🇳 Mainland China resources
Hotline
National Women’s Rights Protection Hotline
The All-China Women’s Federation hotline for reporting domestic violence and seeking support.
📞 12338
Emergency
Police emergency line
Call 110 during domestic violence or another immediate emergency.
📞 110
Legal aid
Beijing Yuanzhong Family and Community Development Service Center
Provides nationwide legal aid, psychological support, and case accompaniment for people affected by gender-based violence.
📞 177-0124-2202
Mental health
National Mental Health Hotline
National Health Commission unified hotline for mental health consultation, support, and crisis intervention. Available nationwide, 24/7.
📞 12356
Legal aid
National Legal Aid Hotline
Ministry of Justice public legal-service hotline for legal consultation and information about legal aid. Available nationwide, 24/7.
📞 12348
Law
Anti-Domestic Violence Law of the People’s Republic of China
In force since 2016, covering personal safety protection orders, mandatory reporting, and police responsibilities.
